Privacy Policy

Chronicle Cryptogram · Effective August 19, 2026

Chronicle Cryptogram is a daily cryptogram game. This policy describes what we collect and why. We do not sell personal information and we do not show ads.

Account

Sign-in is optional and uses Google. When you sign in we receive your Google account identifier, display name, email address, and profile photo from Google so we can show your agent plate and keep progress on the leaderboard.

Game progress

Puzzle mappings, timers, hints, solves, and related stats are stored on your device in local storage. If you sign in, the same progress and leaderboard stats are stored in Firebase Firestore so they can sync across devices.

Notifications

If you subscribe to paper delivery, the app schedules a local daily reminder on your device. We do not send marketing messages.

What we do not collect

We do not run advertising SDKs, do not sell data, and do not use your puzzles or account for profiling beyond operating the game and leaderboard.

Retention

Local data stays on your device until you clear site data or uninstall the app. Cloud progress remains while your account is used to play. Sign out from the bureau file in the header; contact us if you want cloud records deleted.
